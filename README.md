# Ebay-Sales-Sql-Analysis
SQL analysis of personal eBay e-commerce sales data using SQLite. Includes profit by category, margin analysis, partner splits, and monthly performance.
# BricksAhoy616 eBay Sales Analysis

## Overview
SQL analysis of 46 personal eBay transactions from a self-operated 
LEGO reselling business generating $2,364 in total profit.

## Tools Used
- SQL (SQLite via DB Browser)

## Business Questions Answered
1. Which LEGO category generated the most profit?
2. What price tier has the highest profit margin?
3. How did each partner contribute to total profit?
4. What were the top 5 most profitable individual sales?
5. Which months had the highest sales volume?
6. What are the overall business performance statistics?

## Key Findings
- Ninjago was the top category with $832 total profit
- Under $25 listings had the highest margin at 96.9%
- 46 total sales with $54.92 average profit per sale
- Overall average profit margin of 83%
